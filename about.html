About BookWise SA

Our Story

BookWise SA was founded in 2019 by a group of teachers and librarians in Cape Town. They saw that many schools lacked access to age-appropriate reading materials and decided to take action.

What started as a small book collection drive in one community has grown into a registered NPO serving 20 schools across the Western Cape. We have donated over 5,000 books and trained more than 100 volunteers to run reading programmes.

We believe that every child deserves the opportunity to become a confident reader and that access to books is a fundamental right, not a luxury.

Our Mission

To promote literacy and a love for reading by providing books and reading resources to under-resourced schools in South Africa.

Our Vision

A South Africa where every child has access to books and the opportunity to become a confident reader.

Our Core Values

Accessibility

We believe books should be available to every child, regardless of their family's financial situation.

Education

We are committed to improving educational outcomes through literacy.

Community

We work with schools, families, and local organisations to create sustainable change.

Integrity

We are accountable to our donors, volunteers, and the communities we serve.
