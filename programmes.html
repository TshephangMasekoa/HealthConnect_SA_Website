Free Health Programmes for Communities

We offer three programmes designed to improve health outcomes in under-resourced communities. All our programmes are free for participants.

Flagship Programme

Health Workshops

Weekly sessions | 2 hours per session | All materials provided

We run health education workshops in communities covering topics like nutrition, hygiene, disease prevention, and first aid. Workshops are facilitated by qualified health workers and volunteers.

What participants learn:

  • Basic nutrition and healthy eating
  • Hygiene practices for disease prevention
  • First aid and emergency response
  • Understanding common diseases

Current Locations:

Diepsloot, Alexandra, Tembisa, and surrounding areas

Healthcare Access

Clinic Connections

Ongoing | Connecting communities to local clinics

We help community members find and access local clinics. Our team provides information about clinic locations, operating hours, and services available. We also assist with referrals for specialised care.

How it works:

  • For community members: Find your nearest clinic and learn about available services
  • For clinics: Partner with us to reach more community members
  • For volunteers: Help us connect people to healthcare services

Impact to date:

15 partner clinics, 5,000+ community members reached

Youth Focus

Youth Health Programme

School-based | 6-week programme | Ages 15-35

We provide health education to young people on topics like mental health, sexual health, and substance abuse. Our programme is delivered in schools and community centres.

Topics covered:

  • Mental health and well-being
  • Sexual and reproductive health
  • Substance abuse awareness
  • Healthy relationships
  • Nutrition and physical activity
