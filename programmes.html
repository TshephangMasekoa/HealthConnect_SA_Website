Free Literacy Programmes for Schools

We offer three programmes designed to promote literacy and a love for reading. All our programmes are free for participating schools.

Flagship Programme

Reading Clubs

Weekly sessions | 2 hours per week | Ages 6-12

Our reading clubs are designed to help children discover the joy of reading in a fun, supportive environment. Each session includes read-aloud activities, group discussions, and individual reading time.

What children gain:

  • Improved reading and comprehension skills
  • Confidence in their reading ability
  • Access to a wide variety of books
  • A love for reading

Current Locations:

Six schools in Cape Town, two in Stellenbosch, and one in Paarl

Donation Programme

Book Drives

Ongoing | Collecting new and used books

We collect books from individuals, schools, and corporate partners and distribute them to schools in need. Books are sorted by age group and reading level before distribution.

How it works:

  • For donors: Donate new or gently used books (all ages)
  • For schools: Apply to receive books for your library or classroom
  • For learners: Books are provided free of charge

Impact to date:

Over 5,000 books distributed to 20 schools

Teacher Training

Literacy Workshops

4-week course | 3 hours per week

We equip teachers with strategies to improve reading outcomes in their classrooms. Workshops cover phonics, comprehension strategies, and creating a reading culture in schools.

Course modules include:

  • Reading assessment and tracking
  • Phonics and decoding strategies
  • Comprehension and critical thinking
  • Creating a classroom library
  • Engaging parents in reading
